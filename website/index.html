FAQ

Questions businesses ask us

Are you insured?

Yes — full public liability insurance, and all staff are police-checked. Certificates of currency are available on request.

Do you clean outside business hours?

Most of our commercial clients prefer early-morning, evening or overnight cleans. We build the schedule around your operating hours.

How is pricing determined?

Every quote follows a site inspection. We price on the actual scope — area, frequency, fit-out and standard required — rather than a generic rate card.

Do we get the same cleaners each time?

Yes. We assign a consistent team to your site so they learn your layout, your standards and your access procedures.

What happens if something isn't right?

Every clean ends with a quality check against your site checklist. If anything is missed, it's corrected before sign-off — and you have a direct line to us, not a call centre.

Which areas do you service?

All of metropolitan Adelaide — CBD, north, south, east, west and the Adelaide Hills. We're based in St Agnes.
